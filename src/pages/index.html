THE CREATOR

I'm an independent cartoon, comic and illustration artist creating dynamic illustrations, memorable characters, and exciting visual stories. At least that's what Google told me when I searched for my name!

Anyway, in reality I'm a 64 year old man who only started taking drawing seriously 5 years ago. Sharing this journey to have a table at a Comic Con in 2027 is my way of proving you can achieve ANYTHING at ANY Age. If the Rolling Stones are still rocking in their 80's then I can fulfil my goal (and so can you). Watch me succedd (and fail repeatedly) through the Blog and the Microcast.
